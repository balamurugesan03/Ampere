Privacy Policy

Ampere mobile app & services · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ampere ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use the Ampere mobile application (Android package com.ampereapp.mobile) and related services (together, the "Service"). By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number and password (stored only in encrypted/hashed form).
  • Delivery addresses: contact name, phone number, address line, city, state and PIN code.
  • Orders: products purchased, quantities, prices, coupons applied, delivery slot, order status and order history.
  • Payment information: your chosen payment method (UPI QR or Cash on Delivery), payment status and any transaction reference you provide. We do not collect or store your card numbers, UPI PIN or bank login credentials. UPI payments are completed in your own UPI/banking app.
  • Referral & rewards details: your referral code, the sponsor who referred you, your team/network information, PV (point value), rank, rewards points, wallet balance, commissions and payout records (amount, method and reference).
  • Preferences: wishlist, cart contents and notification settings.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical information such as IP address, device/browser type and request logs, used for security and troubleshooting.
  • A login token stored on your device so you stay signed in.

We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To process, deliver and support your orders, including verifying payments.
  • To run the referral/rewards programme: calculating PV, ranks, commissions, wallet balances and payouts.
  • To send you service notifications (order updates, rewards, offers) – you can turn these off in the app.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and abuse, and to keep the Service secure.
  • To comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • To improve our products and Service.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • Within your referral network: limited details (such as your name, referral code, rank and PV) may be visible to your sponsor/upline and team as part of the rewards programme.
  • With service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g. cloud hosting, database and delivery partners), only as needed and under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law, court order or government authority, or to protect the rights and safety of Ampere, our users or others.
  • In a business transfer: if Ampere is merged, acquired or sells its assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Data storage & security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Passwords are hashed, access to the Service is protected with authentication tokens, and administrative access is restricted to authorised staff. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Order, payment, commission and payout records may be kept longer where required for legal, tax, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.

6. Your rights & choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and update your profile and addresses in the app.
  • Notifications: you can turn notifications on or off in the app settings.
  • Account deletion: you can request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us (see section 10). We will delete or anonymise your data, except records we must keep by law.
  • Grievances: you may raise any concern about your data with us, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian laws.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-party links & apps

When you pay through a UPI app or open external links, that third party's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for their practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this link, with the "Last updated" date shown above. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
